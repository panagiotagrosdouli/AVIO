# AVIO Robotics — startup thesis

Independent pre-incorporation deep-tech initiative. SkEyes is scientific inspiration, not an affiliation or a source of proprietary technology.

## Mission
Help civilian emergency response teams gain timely situational awareness through collaborative aerial perception.

## Initial product: AVIO Response
A software-first mission replay and shared mapping prototype for three simulated UAVs, with human supervision. Initial scope: post-disaster mapping in a controlled simulated environment. No real-flight, victim-detection, safety or commercial readiness claims.

## Customer discovery
Interview 10–15 civilian emergency response coordinators, drone service providers and civil protection professionals. Investigate current mapping workflows, data handoff, procurement, connectivity and operational constraints. Seek two nonbinding design-partner discussions. Do not represent interviews or pilots as completed.

## Business model hypotheses
Annual software license per organization or fleet; paid integration and training; pilot contracts after validation. Pricing and willingness to pay are unknown.

## First 90 days
- Days 1–30: customer interviews, reproducible shared occupancy mapping baseline, architecture and requirements; identify IP/license constraints.
- Days 31–60: compare independent versus shared mapping with identical sensing budgets, multiple seeds and simulated communication faults; display recorded results in dashboard.
- Days 61–90: demonstrate a controlled simulated disaster mapping workflow and collect structured feedback; prepare a pilot proposal and investor brief.

## Evidence gates
Coverage, map precision/recall, communication overhead, mission completion time, reproducibility and limitations. Simulated collision monitoring is not a validated flight safety system. Physical trials require separate expert safety and applicable regulatory review.

## Founding team needs
Robotics/SLAM, software and simulation, domain expertise/customer discovery. Assign ownership only when people actually join.

## Funding readiness
Maintain a budget, IP register, reproducible demo, customer interview evidence and a milestones-based funding ask. Verify current grant eligibility before applying.
