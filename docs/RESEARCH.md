## Research protocol
1. Read original paper and authoritative project documentation.
2. Maintain full bibliographic attribution and version identifiers.
3. Record source-code and asset licenses before download or reuse.
4. Prefer AVIO-authored independent implementations and benchmark against published methods.
5. Clearly distinguish reproduced results, original experiments, and unvalidated concepts.
